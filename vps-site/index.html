Storage & usage

Files download to /data/botty/downloads/incomplete and move to /data/botty/downloads/complete when finished. They are not added to your game library automatically.

Check available storage in Transmission before adding a large download. Keep the PS5 awake. Use Stop safely before shutting down; resume after the next session with this button.

The temporary launcher may add a Homebrew Launcher icon. Its port 8080 is closed after startup. Transmission uses a password and allows localhost and 192.168.x.x clients. Do not forward console ports.
