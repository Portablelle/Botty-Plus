# Botty+ 2.0.2

The embedded Switch engine (still ProsperoEden 1.005.000) gains five fixes ported
from PS5-ECAM:

- Mods and cheats in a game's folder apply to that game only, not to its update
  or DLC. A game whose disabled mod still reached its DLC data, such as Mario
  Kart 8 Deluxe with the Booster Course, starts again.
- With several updates beside a game, its version and Control data come from the
  newest update, like its code.
- A full audio queue drops the new audio buffer instead of ending the game.
- Games made of several programs, such as Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection,
  can switch to their next program.
- A shader the graphics driver refuses no longer crashes the engine; its draws
  are skipped and the reason is logged.

Existing games, saves and settings are preserved. These are build-validated
changes; PS5 gameplay acceptance remains separate.
